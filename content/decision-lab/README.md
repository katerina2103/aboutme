# Decision Lab slide content

The numbered YAML files follow the live slide order.

1. Your decision
2. Scope the problem (external evidence and internal discovery)
3. Market comparison
4. Strategic trade-offs
5. Pilot plan and funding ask (`05-value-and-reuse.yaml`, under `sequence`)
6. Simulated pilot outcomes and decision (`06-funding-recommendation.yaml`, under `sequence`)
7. Vision, strategy, roadmap, and vendor P&L (`07-custom-product-plan.yaml`, under `vendor_plan`)
8. Celebration

Edit the text in these files. Slide 2's `copy` contains the live research plan, and `evidence` contains the references and assumptions.

Company context, answer feedback and shared controls live in `../06-decision-lab.yaml`. Slide layouts, table labels and interactive calculation logic also live in `src/_includes/slides/` and `src/_includes/decision-lab.js`. Run `npm run build` after editing.
