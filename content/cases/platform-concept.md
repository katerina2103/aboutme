## The opportunity

Explore how Decisioning platform Customer Decision Hub could connect with BI tools, using existing analytics data and making decisioning data available for analysis.

## The proposed response

Combine API access, certified data-source connections and embedded dashboards. The proposed MVP focused on narrower Tableau Server and Next-Best-Action use cases.

## Commercial and operational reasoning

The deck considered customer overlap, subscription pricing, expected cloud operations costs and reuse of existing capabilities. These were proposal assumptions, not validated commercial results.

## Making the experience tangible

Low-fidelity connector screens explored selecting a BI service, authenticating, testing connectivity and choosing certified sources.

## What I would test next

Proposed next step: validate the customer problem and demand with potential users, then test a narrow integration and measure its usefulness before expanding scope.
