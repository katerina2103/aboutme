Approach / How I work

Good questions.
Useful experiments.
Shared progress.

My background in science taught me to work with uncertainty. Product work taught me that evidence only helps when people can use it to make a decision.

Explore my 14 principles ↗

Understand before prescribing.

Get close to the actual work. Find its purpose, what already works and what causes friction.

Question with a better alternative.

Challenge assumptions constructively. Make the tradeoffs visible and substantiate the value.

Make the next decision useful.

Turn strategic opportunities into focused experiments, with committed people and clear learning goals.

Bring people into the change.

Share knowledge generously. Invite different perspectives and connect participation to action.

About / A little more Katya

A wide lens.
A practical streak.

“I can happily nerd out on anything from finance and taxes to art history, science and data.”

I started in science, moved through analytics and found my way into product management. The part I enjoy most has stayed the same: getting underneath messy problems, understanding how the pieces connect, and making something useful to others.

I’ve spent 16 years living across Europe, from Austria and Germany to the Netherlands. Different perspectives and ways of thinking are part of what makes the work interesting.
